✦ About Her ✦

A Teacher Beyond the Classroom

Miss Samah is more than a teacher — she is a guide, a mentor, and a source of endless inspiration. With her warm presence and genuine care, she transforms every lesson into a memorable journey.

Her passion for the English language is contagious. She has the rare gift of making complex ideas feel simple and every student feel capable and valued.

Her classroom is a safe haven where mistakes become lessons, questions are celebrated, and every student discovers their own voice.
